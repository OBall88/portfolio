Guys and Dolls · Promotional film

The King’s Academy
Guys and Dolls

The video was a promotional and a teaser for The King's Academy's upcoming production of Guys and Dolls

My contribution

I was in charge of everything other than shooting the actual head shots. I created the vfx like the dice effect, and use green screen keying to create moving backgrounds. Basically everything that wasn't recording the head shots.

Inside the film / In my words

01 / The effect

Beyond the frame

I made the decision to use VFX to make it look like a pair of dice comes out of the screen

The head shots are the primary piece of the video, so I have to be intentional about picking elements that complement the characters instead of distracting from them

02 / Creative direction

Character comes first

I used elements that directly apply to the characters that were featured.

03 / Reflection

Built from scratch

The video was custom made from scratch, no pre-existing template. So piecing together all of the moving elements from scratch took a long time.
