Guys and Dolls · Promotional film

The King’s Academy
Guys and Dolls

A promotional teaser for The King’s Academy’s production of Guys and Dolls.

My contribution

I handled post-production, including editing, visual effects, green-screen keying, and animated backgrounds. The cast headshots were filmed by others.

Prefer another screen? Watch on Vimeo ↗

Inside the film / Creative process

01 / The effect

Beyond the frame

I created a visual effect that makes a pair of dice appear to come out of the screen, adding a more engaging, interactive feel.

The headshots are the focus. Every moving element needed to complement the characters without distracting from them.

02 / Creative direction

Character comes first

I selected visual elements connected to each featured character, giving the animation a clear purpose within the teaser.

03 / Reflection

Built from scratch

I built the video from scratch without a pre-existing template. Assembling and coordinating all of the moving elements was time-intensive and required deliberate attention to how each piece supported the cast.
