Anniversary film

SCLA
20th Anniversary

It was to commemorate the 20th Anniversary of the Supply Chain Leaders in Action collective. They wanted to celebrate the people who help build the group, the things that they have impacted, the challenges they had to over come, and what the future looks like.

My contribution

I made the entire video myself

Prefer another screen? Watch on Vimeo ↗

Inside the film / In my words

i wanted to make something cinematic but also nostalgic.

01 / The audience

A shared history

The people who had built the community were going to see it, and I wanted them to feel nostalgic about the old days, but have it viewed in a way that they were a part of something bigger than themselves.

Sound

I used sound design and epic music to build tension.

Images

I used old photos from the early 2000s of the original groups meeting.

Voice

I then used the SCLA President to create a voice over to carry along the story of the video

02 / Reflection

Beyond the screen

I've learned that people want to feel important. When you create a project for someone that has a personal connection, making the video larger than life gives them a sense of pride and fulfillment. Now my video has become a reference point for their career
