Anniversary film

SCLA
20th Anniversary

This film commemorates the 20th anniversary of Supply Chain Leaders in Action, celebrating the people who built the community, its impact, the challenges it has overcome, and its vision for the future.

My contribution

I handled the full video production.

Prefer another screen? Watch on Vimeo ↗

Inside the film / Creative process

I wanted to create something cinematic, but also nostalgic.

01 / The audience

A shared history

The audience included the people who helped build the community. I wanted to reconnect them with its early days while showing how their contributions became part of something larger than themselves.

Sound

I combined sound design with an epic musical score to build tension.

Images

Archival photographs from the early 2000s brought the group’s first meetings into the story.

Voice

A voice-over recorded with the SCLA president carried the narrative through the film.

02 / Reflection

Beyond the screen

I learned how much it matters for people to see the value of their contributions. Giving a personally meaningful story a larger-than-life treatment can create a sense of pride and fulfillment. This film became a reference point for the careers it celebrates.
